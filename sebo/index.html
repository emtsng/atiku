01How to follow it

Four instructions already on the note, written as a daily order.

  1. Build breakfast as the cocktail: blend it into a smoothie, or eat it as a salad.
  2. At each main meal, choose one staple and keep to the stated portion.
  3. Vegetables are unrestricted. Meat, chicken, or fish is 50 g.
  4. Take the supplements for one month, and tick each dose on the day you take it.

Ticks are saved in this browser only. They are not sent anywhere.

Blend as a smoothie, or eat as a salad. Tick each item as you add it.

04Staples, one per meal

Pick one food for the meal and stop at the portion. Grams are the dietitian’s figures. Only spaghetti and awara were given as household measures.

05Two soups

Both recipes use African yam bean and fenugreek. Tick the soup you make.

Soup 1 Bitter leaf (Vernonia amygdalina) + scent leaf (Ocimum gratissimum) + African yam bean + fenugreek.
Soup 2 Garden egg leaf + utazi leaf (Gongronema latifolium) + African yam bean + fenugreek.

06Fruit portions

Each fruit on the note is paired with “A”. Carrot is written as 2 + 1A. The note does not spell out what A is.

Confirm with the dietitian what “A” means before treating it as a second food. It is copied here exactly as it appears on the note.

07Vegetables and protein

Vegetables: no restriction on the note. Use them with the staple and with either soup.
